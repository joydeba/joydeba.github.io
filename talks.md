# Talks, Posters & Conference Visits

### CASCON 2025 (Toronto, Canada)
Conference talk on the technical debt fix trade off in software backporting (IEEE CASCON 2025 paper with Tasnim, Roy, and Schneider). Supported by Saskatchewan Polytechnic Professional Development funding.

### ICSE 2023 (Melbourne, Australia)
Poster presentation *How Quality Deviates in Stable Releases through Backporting* (with Tasnim, Roy, and Schneider). Attended the main conference and co located events (14 to 20 May 2023). Graduate travel award support.

### SEMLA 2022 (Montréal, Canada)
Poster presentation *Backporting for Version Management* at Polytechnique Montréal (Best Poster, 3rd). Graduate travel award support.
