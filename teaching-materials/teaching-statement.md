## 1. Teaching Philosophy

My teaching is grounded in experiential learning, inclusive pedagogy, and systems thinking. Students learn computing most effectively when they connect theory with hands-on practice, critical reflection, and real-world applications. My goal is to help learners become ethical, adaptive professionals who can design reliable, maintainable, and socially responsible systems.

I treat the classroom as an active, collaborative space. Students are co-creators of knowledge who question, experiment, and learn with peers. I use outcomes-based design, staged assignments, and structured formative feedback so learners understand both how systems work and how to improve them thoughtfully.

## 2. Teaching Experience

As an Instructor in the School of Computing and Digital Innovation at Saskatchewan Polytechnic (Regina Campus), I teach and design materials across Computer Systems Technology and Cloud Computing & Blockchain, including systems administration, networking, web applications, cloud platforms, DevOps, and project-oriented courses. I have also rewritten course and lab materials for most courses I teach.

As Lecturer in CSE at Khulna University (2013–2017; later on study leave), I taught Structured Programming, Software Development Project, Advanced Programming Laboratory, and Software Engineering and Information System. I also taught Computer Science courses for the BA program at North Western University (2014–2016) and ICT courses for high school teachers at HSTTI, Khulna (2014–2016).

## 3. Teaching Practices and Pedagogical Innovation

**Learn by doing.** Courses center on labs, design projects, and authentic workflows—for example deploying cloud services, building secure web applications, practicing version control and testing pipelines, and completing milestone-based team projects.

**Inclusive and culturally responsive pedagogy.** Completing the Reconciliation Education Certificate and Four Seasons of Reconciliation informs transparent expectations, multimodal resources, equitable access to assessments, and a classroom culture centered on respect and collaboration.

**Contemporary tools and AI-assisted development.** I keep materials current with CI/CD practices, cloud and systems tooling, and responsible use of AI-assisted developer workflows, so students build both conceptual depth and professional readiness.

## 4. Mentorship and Student Engagement

Mentorship is an extension of teaching. I have mentored an M.Sc. student on software backporting and quality analysis (co-authored publications), supervised undergraduate summer research students, guided teaching assistants, and supported student presentations at venues such as the SOAR Symposium. Mentorship emphasizes self-directed learning, ethical reasoning, and reflective practice.

## 5. Teaching Impact

My long-term commitment is to prepare students who are thoughtful, responsible, and technically strong. Through active learning, inclusive pedagogy, curriculum design, and sustained instructional development (including LIFT and New Instructor Orientation), I aim to create classrooms that are intellectually engaging, welcoming to diverse learners, technically rigorous, and grounded in practice.
