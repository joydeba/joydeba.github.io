\noindent Dear Members of the Search Committee,

I am writing to apply for the Assistant Professor position in Computer Science / Software Engineering. I am an educator and computer scientist with a Ph.D.\ from the University of Saskatchewan (2024) and a sustained record of university and polytechnic teaching, curriculum design, and student mentorship. I look forward to contributing high-quality instruction, inclusive classroom practice, and research-informed courses while supporting student success and departmental teaching needs.

I bring a strong record of teaching and curriculum development. I currently teach in the School of Computing and Digital Innovation at Saskatchewan Polytechnic (Computer Systems Technology; Cloud Computing \& Blockchain), where I design and rewrite course and lab materials across systems, networking, web applications, cloud platforms, DevOps, and project-oriented courses. I recently served as Sessional Lecturer for CMPT~145 at the University of Saskatchewan with full course responsibility and guidance of three teaching assistants. Earlier, as Lecturer in CSE at Khulna University, I taught programming, software engineering, and related courses, and completed extensive pedagogical training, including Foundation Training for University Teachers. My teaching emphasizes experiential learning, inclusive pedagogy, and contemporary practice---labs, projects, cloud/DevOps tooling, and responsible AI-assisted development. I am prepared to teach core courses in programming, software engineering, and systems, as well as topics such as software maintenance, mining software repositories, and AI for software engineering.

My research in AI-assisted software maintenance strengthens my teaching. Under Professors Chanchal~K.~Roy and Kevin Schneider, I developed Baczer, ReBack, BackSlice, and BackTrans for automated software backporting, with publications in venues including ICSE, ICPC, MSR, CASCON, and ASE Journal. As Living Skies Postdoctoral Fellow and AI Automation Consultant with Cloud Linux Software, Inc., I connect research to practice that I bring into the classroom. I have mentored an M.Sc.\ student (with co-authored publications), supervised undergraduate summer researchers, and guided teaching assistants.

I am committed to service and equity-minded education, including as President of the Computer Science Graduate Council at the University of Saskatchewan, and I have completed Reconciliation Education training. I value collaborative academic cultures and look forward to contributing to departmental service, curriculum development, and student success.

Enclosed please find my curriculum vitae, teaching statement, and research statement. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my teaching and scholarly experience can support your department's mission. Thank you for your consideration.
