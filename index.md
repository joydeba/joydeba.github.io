Welcome! I am **Debasish Chakroborti (Joy), Ph.D.**, a researcher and educator in **AI-assisted software maintenance**. My goal is **issue-free software on the versions people actually use**.

I work on legacy-system monitoring, technical debt management, continuous maintenance, cybersecurity maintenance, resource optimization, and software sustainability — often with industry collaborators. I also teach in the **Computer System Technology** and **Cloud Computing & Blockchain** programs at Saskatchewan Polytechnic, and continue research as a **Postdoctoral Fellow** at the University of Saskatchewan.
